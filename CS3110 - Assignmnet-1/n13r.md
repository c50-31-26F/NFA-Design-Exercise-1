# Problem 13 NFA Report

## Language

This NFA recognizes strings over the alphabet `{0,1}` that contain at least 2 1's

Examples of accepted strings include: '11', '011', '111 ', '1010' 

Examples of rejected strings include `0`, `1`, `000`, `100`.
